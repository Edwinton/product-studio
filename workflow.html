AI content creation system · Task 3

From raw idea to publish-ready carousel in under an hour

A repeatable pipeline that lets the founder produce carousels alone. AI does the research, structuring, copy and layout. The founder makes the decisions that matter: the angle, the facts and the final sign-off.

The pipeline

  1. 1

    Capture idea

    Founder drops a raw note into a form or chat.

    Google Form / Notion
  2. 2

    Research & structure

    AI pulls sources and outlines 5 to 7 slides.

    Claude + web search
  3. 3

    Copy & headlines

    Headlines and slide copy, with 3 options each.

    Claude / ChatGPT
  4. ✓

    Founder checkpoint

    Pick the angle, check facts, approve the copy.

    Human review
  5. 4

    Layout & visuals

    Brand template fills with approved copy.

    Canva brand kit / Figma
  6. 5

    Export & schedule

    Slides export as PNG or PDF and queue to the calendar.

    Make / n8n + Buffer

Design principles

Human at the decision points

AI proposes; the founder chooses the angle and approves facts. This protects brand trust.

Templates before prompts

A fixed brand kit keeps every carousel consistent, so quality doesn’t depend on prompt wording.

Repeatable by design

Each run stores its outline, copy and export in one folder, so any post can be redone in minutes.
